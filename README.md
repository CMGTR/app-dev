# *Dredd* (2012)

**Dredd** is a **sci-fi action film** directed by **Pete Travis** and written by **Alex Garland**, based on the long-running *Judge Dredd* comic series from *2000 AD*.  

---

## Overview

Set in a **dystopian future**, the world is overrun by crime and chaos.  
The only force keeping order is the **Judges** — law enforcers who serve as **judge, jury, and executioner**.  

---

## Plot Summary

In the mega-city known as **Mega-City One**, **Judge Dredd** and rookie **Judge Anderson**, a psychic, are sent to investigate a triple homicide in a 200-story slum tower called **Peach Trees**.

Inside, they find themselves trapped and hunted by the ruthless gang leader **Ma-Ma**, who controls the distribution of a dangerous drug called **Slow-Mo**, which slows down the user’s perception of time to a crawl.

---

## Main Characters

| Character | Actor | Role |
|------------|--------|------|
| Judge Dredd | Karl Urban | A stoic and skilled law enforcer |
| Judge Anderson | Olivia Thirlby | A rookie Judge with psychic powers |
| Ma-Ma | Lena Headey | A brutal gang leader who rules Peach Trees |

---

## Iconic Quote

> “Judgment time.” — *Judge Dredd*  

---

## Why *Dredd* Stands Out

1. **Gritty cyberpunk aesthetic** that feels raw and immersive.  
2. **Stylized slow-motion visuals** powered by the “Slo-Mo” drug effect.  
3. **Tight, self-contained plot** — all action, no filler.  
4. **Karl Urban’s performance** perfectly captures Dredd’s stoic intensity.
